Privacy and your documents
Updated: October 15, 2026

Capture, edge detection, cropping, image enhancement, drawing and PDF creation run on your phone. The app does not require an account and has no ads or usage analytics. The app has no Internet permission.

Camera access is used when you choose to capture a page. Import uses the system picker to access only your selected image, without broad gallery access. A private copy is kept for processing and manual cropping. Your original gallery image is not changed.

Your draft, original photos and drawings are stored in the app's private storage so you can return after switching apps. They are excluded from the app's automatic backup. Start over or explicitly Exit deletes the draft. Uninstalling the app deletes its private data.

Save to phone creates a copy in Documents. Copies already saved there remain when you start over or exit.

Sharing and cloud saving happen only when you choose them, through an external app or storage provider. That service receives access to the selected export and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Cloud sync and message delivery may require that service's Internet connection.

Temporary shared files are in the app's private cache, which Android may clear. You can clear the cache in system settings. Clearing app data also deletes the draft.

At first launch, the app reads the country code reported by the registered mobile network to choose Hebrew for Israel or English otherwise. If unavailable, English is used. Only the language choice is saved. No GPS location, SIM identifiers or phone number are requested, and the country code is not sent anywhere. The choice is kept when you travel.

Check readability and cropping before saving or sharing. Image processing can change the appearance of text and does not replace keeping your original.
